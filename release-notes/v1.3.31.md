# NomaTune v1.3.31

## Dock: the backgrounds were never given anything to blur

This was the missing piece behind all three problems you reported at once.

ArchiveTune applies `Modifier.hazeSource(...)` to its scrolling content, which registers that content as the thing the
floating bars sample. NomaTune created a haze state and passed it to the dock, but **never registered any source** -
there were zero `hazeSource` calls in the whole app, against six in ArchiveTune.

With no source, every background style has no input:

- **Connected and Standalone looked identical** - both fell back to the same flat tint.
- **Apple Music looked invisible** - its glass layer had nothing to sample, so it drew nothing.

Now the NavHost content and the search overlay are both registered as haze sources, exactly where ArchiveTune puts
them. `FloatingNavigationToolbar`, `AppleMusicNavigationToolbar` and `FloatingBarGlassBackground` were already
byte-identical to ArchiveTune - they just had no input.

## Home screen

Compared section by section against ArchiveTune's `HomeScreenComponents.kt` (2262 lines vs NomaTune's 1305):

- Ported **`HomeCategoryChips`** - ArchiveTune renders the category row as `ToggleButton` pills in a horizontal
  scroller. NomaTune was using the older `ChipsRow`, which is why the row under the app title looked different.
- Ported **`HomeSectionHeader`** for section headings.
- Section spacing now matches ArchiveTune's `HomeSectionSpacing` rhythm.

Still missing and tracked for the next build: `RemoteQuickPicksSection` (the remote "Quick picks" hero carousel) and
`AccountPlaylistsSection` / `SimilarRecommendationsSection` - these need `HomeViewModel` state NomaTune does not
expose yet.
